Join UDSF What we do

Students who listen, organise and follow through, so every voice at GEC Wayanad is heard where it counts.

  • Student welfare

    Hostel, canteen, transport and fees, taken to the administration.

  • Academic concerns

    Exams, results, labs and KTU issues, followed until fixed.

  • College union

    Putting students who listen into the seats that decide.

  • Campaigns

    Conventions and events that bring the campus together.

The alliance

Two organisations. One front.

KSU

Kerala Students Union

The student organisation of the Congress movement in Kerala, active on campuses across the state for generations.

+

MSF

Muslim Students Federation

The student wing of the Indian Union Muslim League, with a strong presence in colleges throughout Kerala.
